Renting a scooter
  • Price: about IDR 60,000 to 100,000 a day for a 110cc scooter, which is roughly PKR 1,000 to 1,650. This is from one 2026 guide, so ask two shops.
  • Licence: Indonesian law needs your home licence plus an International Driving Permit that covers motorcycles. Get the permit in Pakistan before you fly.
  • Fines: about IDR 300,000 to 500,000 for each missing document, and IDR 250,000 for no helmet.
  • Insurance: check your travel policy. Many do not pay for a crash if you ride without the right licence.
  • Where it fits: the short days in Canggu, Uluwatu and Seminyak. Use the driver for Ubud, Mt Batur and the moving days.
  • Nights out: take a Grab home. Never ride after drinking.

The pink scooter pins are rental shops listed on OpenStreetMap near each stay. Their prices and reviews are not checked, so read the Google reviews first.

Google Maps mode (needs your key)

With your own Google Maps key this page adds Google's 3D view, plus photos, ratings and exact addresses when you tap a place. Google charges each map load and place lookup to your Cloud credits.

  1. Open console.cloud.google.com and pick or create a project with billing on.
  2. Enable three APIs: Maps JavaScript API, Places API (New) and Map Tiles API.
  3. Go to Credentials, create an API key, and restrict it to those three APIs.
  4. Paste the key below. It is saved only in this browser.
